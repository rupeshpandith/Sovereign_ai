# Document Deliverable Generator Skill

## Purpose

Generate real business deliverables, not just chat responses.

## Required Outputs

- Approval notes as DOCX
- Calculation sheets as XLSX
- Summary decks as PPTX
- Evidence reports as PDF/HTML

## Approval Note Structure

1. Title
2. Background
3. Inspection summary
4. Key findings
5. SOP/manual evidence
6. Risk/impact
7. Recommendation
8. Human approval section
9. Evidence table

## Constraints

- Do not invent measurements.
- Do not invent dates.
- Use placeholders if source data is missing.
- Include evidence references where possible.
