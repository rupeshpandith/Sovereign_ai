STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-22
Title    : Valve Inspection and Leak Testing Procedures for High-Pressure Service
Revision : Rev 2   |   Effective: 2024-06-01   |   Review Due: 2026-06-01
Approved : Chief Inspector, Instrumentation & Piping (signature on file)
Applies to: All control valves and isolation valves in HP steam, HP gas,
            and process services (design pressure > 10 bar), including
            but not limited to: V-33, V-41, V-55, V-102, V-118.

========================================================================

1. PURPOSE

   This SOP defines inspection intervals, leak test methods, acceptance
   criteria, and corrective action requirements for high-pressure valves
   to ensure safe and reliable operation.

2. SCOPE

   §2.1  Covers in-situ inspection, partial-stroke testing, and bench
         testing of control valves and isolation valves in HP service.

   §2.2  V-33 (HP steam letdown, service line SL-06) is a designated
         critical valve requiring Class V seat tightness.

3. INSPECTION INTERVALS

   §3.1  Routine (in-situ walkaround): monthly.
   §3.2  Detailed inspection (this procedure): every 6 months, or
         after any process upset or emergency closure.
   §3.3  Bench teardown and refacing: as determined by §5.1 acceptance
         criteria, or minimum every 4 years.

4. LEAK TEST PROCEDURE (IN-SITU)

   §4.1  Isolate valve and equalise to test pressure (10 bar minimum).
   §4.2  Connect calibrated bubble meter to downstream vent.
   §4.3  Record leakage rate over three 2-minute runs. Report the average.
   §4.4  Annotate report with instrument serial number and calibration date.

5. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA — SEAT LEAKAGE

   §5.1  ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class V (HP steam, V-33 and similar):
         Maximum allowable: 0.30 mL/min per mm of port diameter at
         differential pressure equal to maximum rated pressure.
         — Within limit:   NORMAL, continue service.
         — 0.30–0.50 mL/min (FLAG):  Increased monitoring (4-hourly);
           approval note required per §6.3 before continued operation.
         — > 0.50 mL/min (REJECT):   Immediate isolation and work order
           for bench refacing or trim replacement.

   §5.2  ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class IV (general process valves):
         Maximum allowable: 0.01% of rated Cv per unit differential.

6. CORRECTIVE ACTIONS AND APPROVALS

   §6.1  Work order must be raised for any finding that exceeds acceptance
         criteria, referencing this SOP section and the inspection report ID.

   §6.2  Interim continued operation (FLAG range per §5.1) is permitted
         only if: (a) leakage rate is checked every 4 hours, (b) rate has
         not trended upward over the prior two readings, and (c) an approval
         note has been filed per §6.3.

   §6.3  Approval Note requirement: AN must reference inspection report IR
         number, this SOP (SOP-22), the specific finding number, the measured
         value, and the acceptance limit. AN must be countersigned by the
         Area Engineer before the next inspection shift begins.

7. ACTUATOR AND POSITIONER CHECKS

   §7.1  Positioner deviation must be within ±2 % of demand signal.
   §7.2  Actuator bench-set: verify against valve data sheet (VDS-V33-001
         for V-33: 0.6–1.0 bar).
   §7.3  Packing: zero leakage is the acceptance criterion for all HP valves.

8. REFERENCES

   ANSI/FCI 70-2     — Control valve seat leakage classifications.
   SOP-17            — Vibration limits (for pumps driving HP process valves).
   VDS-V33-001       — Valve data sheet, V-33.
   EM-2022-017       — HP steam service classification list.
