2. FINDING SUMMARY

   Control Valve V-33 (HP steam letdown, service line SL-06, Unit 6) was
   found with in-situ seat leakage of 0.34 mL/min on 2026-07-28, measured
   per SOP-22 §4.3 (three-run average with Fluke 710, Cal due 2026-11-15).

   This exceeds the SOP-22 §5.1 ANSI/FCI 70-2 Class V limit of 0.30 mL/min
   but is below the SOP-22 §5.1 REJECT threshold of 0.50 mL/min.

   Finding F-1 from IR-811: "Seat leakage 0.34 mL/min — exceeds Class V
   limit of 0.30 mL/min. Measurement confirmed as three-run average."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RISK ASSESSMENT

   HP steam letdown valve V-33 is a throttling service with low consequence
   of minor seat leakage on the downstream side; the leakage routes to the
   closed condensate drain header (no atmospheric release).

   Immediate isolation consequence: steam supply interruption to Unit 6
   stripping column; estimated 6-hour restart; production impact ~INR 8L.

   Risk of continued operation: TOLERABLE within the SOP-22 §6.2 condition
   set, provided leakage does not trend upward toward the 0.50 mL/min
   REJECT limit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONDITIONS FOR CONTINUED OPERATION

   (a) Leakage rate measured every 4 hours and recorded in logsheet LS-V33.
   (b) Immediate isolation required if leakage exceeds 0.50 mL/min
       (SOP-22 §5.1 REJECT limit) or shows an increasing trend over two
       consecutive readings.
   (c) WO-2026-3987 progressed to trim replacement within next planned
       maintenance window (target: 2026-08-12).
   (d) Area Engineer to review logsheet daily until WO closed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. APPROVAL DECISION

   APPROVED for continued operation under the conditions in §4 above.
   Validity: until WO-2026-3987 is completed or leakage reaches REJECT
   limit, whichever occurs first.
