   Work Order        : WO-2026-4471 (bearing investigation — raised 2026-08-15)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FINDING SUMMARY

   Equipment P-204 (Centrifugal Feed Pump, Unit 4) was found with Drive
   End (DE) bearing vibration of 6.7 mm/s RMS on 2026-08-15.

   This exceeds the SOP-17 §3.2 ALERT limit of 6.0 mm/s but is below
   the SOP-17 §3.3 SHUTDOWN limit of 7.0 mm/s.

   Finding F-1 from IR-892: "DE vibration at 6.7 mm/s confirmed on three
   consecutive scans over 45 minutes."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. RISK ASSESSMENT

   Current reading (6.7 mm/s) places P-204 in the SOP-17 §3.4 CAUTION
   BAND (> 6.0 and <= 7.0 mm/s). Equipment may operate for a maximum
   of 72 hours in this band with 2-hourly readings.

   Process consequence of immediate shutdown: Column C-101 feed interruption,
   estimated 4-hour recovery, product quality impact estimated at INR 12L.

   Risk of continued operation: LOW for 72-hour window if monitoring is
   maintained; bearing replacement scheduled for next planned shutdown.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CONDITIONS FOR CONTINUED OPERATION

   (a) Vibration readings taken every 2 hours and logged in DCS tag
       PMP-P204-VIB-DE.
   (b) Immediate isolation if reading reaches 7.0 mm/s (SOP-17 §3.3).
   (c) Relief watch notified. Area Engineer alerted for any step-change
       > 0.3 mm/s between consecutive readings.
   (d) WO-2026-4471 progressed to bearing replacement within 72 hours.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. APPROVAL DECISION

   APPROVED for continued operation under the conditions in §4 above,
   for a maximum of 72 hours from the date of this note.
