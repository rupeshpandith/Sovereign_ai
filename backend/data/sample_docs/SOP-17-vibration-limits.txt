STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE — SOP-17
Title    : Vibration Limits and Shutdown Criteria for Rotating Equipment
Revision : Rev 4   |   Effective: 2025-01-01   |   Review Due: 2027-01-01
Approved : Chief Inspector, Mechanical (signature on file)
Applies to: All centrifugal pumps, compressors, and fans in Units 1–8,
            including but not limited to: P-204, P-211, P-115, C-02, FN-07.

========================================================================

1. PURPOSE

   This SOP defines mandatory vibration monitoring frequencies, alert
   thresholds, and shutdown criteria to prevent catastrophic failure of
   rotating machinery and protect personnel safety.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

   §2.1  Applies to all rotating equipment subject to condition-monitoring
         programmes. Heat exchanger associated pumps (e.g. P-211 for HX-12)
         are included under this SOP per Engineering Memo EM-2023-041.

   §2.2  Measurement shall be taken at both Drive End (DE) and Non-Drive
         End (NDE) bearing housings in the radial (horizontal and vertical)
         and axial directions. Report the highest single-axis RMS reading.

   §2.3  Heat exchanger thermal performance checks that include associated
         pump assessments must reference and comply with §3 of this SOP.

3. VIBRATION LIMITS (mm/s RMS — ISO 10816-3 Group 2)

   §3.1  NORMAL (Unrestricted operation):    <= 4.5 mm/s
   §3.2  ALERT  (Increased monitoring):      > 4.5 and <= 6.0 mm/s
           — Readings shall be taken every 4 hours.
           — Engineering supervisor must be notified within 2 hours.
           — An Approval Note (AN) must be raised within 24 hours to
             document risk acceptance for continued operation.
   §3.3  SHUTDOWN (Immediate isolation):     > 7.0 mm/s
           — Equipment must be shut down immediately.
           — No overrides or deferrals are permitted without VP sign-off.
   §3.4  CAUTION BAND (Elevated attention): > 6.0 and <= 7.0 mm/s
           — Equipment may operate for a maximum of 72 hours in this band.
           — Readings every 2 hours. Work order for repair must be raised.
           — AN referencing this SOP section must be filed within 8 hours.

4. MONITORING FREQUENCIES (NORMAL band)

   §4.1  Online continuous monitoring (where installed): real-time alarm
         at the ALERT limit (6.0 mm/s) via DCS.
   §4.2  Monthly route-based handheld check for all pumps without online
         monitoring. Portable calibrated vibration analyser required.
   §4.3  Bi-annual detailed inspection per SOP-17 inspection report template.

5. INSTRUMENT CALIBRATION

   All vibration measurement instruments must be within calibration.
   Calibration records must be cited in every inspection report.

6. REFERENCES

   ISO 10816-3  —  Mechanical vibration, rotating machinery.
   SOP-22       —  Valve Inspection (for valves on pump suction/discharge).
   EM-2023-041  —  Scope extension to heat exchanger associated pumps.
